Users

Add someone

Lets them into Plans: they can create an account (a passkey is quickest) and are emailed how. Only administrators can invite. Everyone's plans and places are completely private to them, and you can't see anyone else's. Adding someone you removed restores them.

People with access

Removing someone only stops them using Plans. What they own stays private and untouched until you delete it, and restoring them puts everything back.

Removed

These people can't sign in to Plans. Deleting their data erases every plan and place they own and can't be undone; their email stays here, removed.
